Multi-brand hospitality platform

A shared digital platform connecting hotel content, booking, loyalty and integrations across properties.

The challenge

A hospitality platform needs to connect the guest journey across properties. Editorial teams publish property content while booking and loyalty services handle distinct business workflows.

Architecture approach

The platform groups content, booking and loyalty as shared capabilities for guest-facing channels. Explicit integration contracts give each capability a clear boundary and help teams coordinate changes across properties.

Operational considerations

Booking and loyalty integrations warrant dedicated monitoring and clearly assigned operational ownership. The guest-facing channels need a way to distinguish a content issue from a booking or integration issue.
